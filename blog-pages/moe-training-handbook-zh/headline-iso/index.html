The Ultimate MoE Training Handbook

How to read this

One training step of a 45-layer MoE on 1,024 GPUs, drawn as a city: 4 pipeline stages, each 64 nodes of 4 GPUs.

  • A square is a GPU: 4 per node, 64 nodes per stage
  • Blue: a micro-batch's forward pass
  • Orange: its backward pass
  • Blue and orange: one of each at once
  • Grey: summing gradients, then the optimizer
  • A dot: a token to its experts and back
  • A line: activations or gradients, stage to stage
  • Hatched: idle time, the pipeline bubble
  • The lens: one node up close, the micro-batches it runs
